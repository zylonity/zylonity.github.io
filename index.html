About me

Hello! I'm Khaleel (also known as Zylonity online), a Computer Science student at Sheffield Hallam University with a background in game design from East London Arts and Music. I'm passionate about creating innovative tech solutions, with experience spanning from software/game development, robotics, and hardware repair.

Key Skills:

  • Programming: C++, C#, Python, Java, Lua
  • Game Development Tools: Unity, Unreal Engine, SFML, DirectX11
  • Design Tools: Photoshop, Autodesk Maya, Blender
  • Operating Systems: Windows, Ubuntu, Debian (Bash terminal, CMD, PowerShell)
  • Hardware Repair: Phones, computers, component-level repair

Experience:
I've developed interactive games in Unity and Unreal Engine and built custom software using Python, C#, and .NET. Additionally, I have hands-on experience with component-level repairs, including fixing phones, computers, and other electronic components, which has honed my troubleshooting skills and deepened my understanding of hardware-software integration. I enjoy solving complex technical problems, whether it's optimizing game mechanics, building efficient systems, or repairing hardware.

In addition to my technical expertise, I have over five years of experience in customer-facing roles, including working at conventions across Europe. These roles have helped me build strong communication and interpersonal skills. Fluent in both English and Spanish, I work effectively with diverse teams and stakeholders.

Currently based between London and Sheffield, I'm open to opportunities worldwide, including remote work. I'm always eager to expand my skills and take on exciting new projects in the tech industry.

Note:
While my portfolio may not fully reflect the breadth of my work, I am continuously involved in new projects and ideas, some of which are restricted by NDAs or privacy requirements.
